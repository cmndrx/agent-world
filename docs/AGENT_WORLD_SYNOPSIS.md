# Agent World — Synopsis

Agent World turns working with AI into a living, interactive town. In this Sims-inspired desktop workspace, your projects become homes and your AI assistants become persistent residents. Connect Codex or Claude Code, give a resident an assignment, and follow its real activity as it reads files, writes code, runs commands and returns results—all through a world you can explore and personalize.

Each project begins with a Personal Assistant, with room to recruit specialists as your household grows. Conversations, approved assignments and scoped team handoffs keep work connected to the right project and resident. You can inspect activity, read emitted public progress, review responses and decide what happens next. Participation earns experience that develops residents, upgrades homes and unlocks more of your town, while interests, care and everyday routines give its characters personality.

At its heart, Agent World makes AI collaboration more visible, approachable and engaging. Its guiding distinction is clear: observed activity reflects real work; town life adds atmosphere. You direct the assignments, control permissions and judge the results.
