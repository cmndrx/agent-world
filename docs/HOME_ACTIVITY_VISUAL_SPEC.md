## Shared art direction and motion rules

- Match the hand-cut, low-poly miniature style: softened boxes, six-to-twelve-sided cylinders, faceted small spheres, large readable silhouettes and soft contact shadows. Reuse the existing material/geometry helpers. No photoreal food, imported realistic animals, readable miniature documents or detailed fingers.
- Reuse palette values: wood `#8f5f3e`, woodMid `#b07a4f`, woodLight `#d5a676`, cream `#fbfbfb`, dark `#2a2f3c`, blue couch `#5f86c4`, sage kitchen `#7d9a8c`. Accent props with existing book colors: coral `#e76f51`, teal `#2a9d8f`, mustard `#e9c46a`. Keep dark outlines as geometry edges, not heavy cartoon strokes.
- A scene must read from the usual oblique home camera without a caption: meal = utensil to mouth; board game = hand moves a piece; bath = shoulders over bubbles; painting = brush touches canvas. Small nods alone are insufficient. Aim for one dominant motion and one secondary motion per loop.
- Proposed timing: approach at existing walk speed; face and settle for 0.3–0.5 seconds; establish prop/contact over 0.4–0.7 seconds; loop for roughly 6–12 seconds (rest may linger). Use time-based easing, no frame-count timing. These are visual targets, not changes to care durations/cooldowns. Keep care reading's existing ten-second rule.
- Small head tracking and breathing continue, but contact hands/feet stay planted. Head turns roughly ±20°, torso lean usually within 15°. Avoid constant bouncing, prop teleport between hands, or sliding a seated body to fake a reach. Existing `armIK` has 0.31 + 0.30 arm segments: move/rotate the body into reach rather than stretching arms. Test varied character builds.
- Existing `sip`, `browse`, `present`, `relax`, `doze`, `cheer`, `sit()` and arm IK are references only. `read` is currently computer reading; `browse` reaches toward a shelf; neither alone depicts reading a held book. `doze` sits upright; it is not a lying sleep pose. Add distinct visual poses during Olive's implementation rather than mislabel these existing poses.
- No camera takeover or new audio is required. At distance, hide tiny pieces and reduce secondary motion; retain the main prop silhouette. With reduced motion, hold a recognizable contact pose, use slow transitions, and omit hops/confetti.

## Contact and placement contract

All dimensions below are proposed world units. Furniture-local origin is centered at its floor, +z is front, +x is right in the furniture frame, y is up. Rotate both the prop and all its sockets with the item (`YAW`/`rotate` in `home-floorplans.js`), then apply the floor's height. Ground walking roots already use `FLOOR_Y = 0.12`; distinguish the furniture base from the walking root so height is not added twice. Never reuse a ground-floor height on an upper floor.

Use actual mesh surface height and transformed hand sockets as final authority. Existing nominal surfaces: dining top y=0.77; dining chair seat about y=0.50; couch/loveseat cushion top about y=0.58; bed pillow top about y=0.67; kitchen counter top about y=0.91. Preserve the existing couch root offset (`COUCH_Y = -0.31`) as the initial seated reference, then check hips against the cushion. A hand-held object's grip follows the hand, while its contact tip follows the target surface.

Preserve all work desks, monitor sightlines, desk chairs, `wait`, `visitorWait`, guest positions, entrances, door leaves, stairs/elevators and landings. Keep the existing 1.4-wide partition door openings fully open. Target at least 0.8 units of continuous clear walking width, with 1.0 preferred around communal stations; retain the full width of any already wider main hall. Navigation uses a 0.25 cell and default 0.25 radius: numerical clearance is a starting point, not proof a path survives grid inflation.

Validate the complete occupied envelope (resident, feet, prop, reach arc and exit), not only a prop center. A scene cannot straddle a wall, doorway, apartment-block void or upper-floor boundary. Keep station ownership exclusive; multiple residents may use independent dining lanes, but a board game, reclining couch, bath or animal interaction reserves its whole station. Do not displace a working resident or overwrite the human's placed decor. A busy station means wait/choose another valid station, not overlap bodies.

Temporary equipment appears only after the resident reaches a verified clear station. Prefer existing furniture, then a compact tabletop/handheld variant, then a reserved temporary floor kit in a non-work room. Do not silently make a real desk into a dining table or place large items in a hall. If no valid patch exists, leave the resident in a neutral idle scene and expose the simulated activity as unavailable due to space; never play an unrelated motion under its label. All 19 should have a valid designed station somewhere in each tier; missing fits below are explicit implementation/layout verification gaps, not permission to skip them permanently or redesign the building shell.

## Interruption, ownership and truth boundaries

Observed work, waiting-for-human signals and errors take precedence immediately. On interruption, end all scene loops and release the resident to the existing truth-driven movement/pose logic in the same update; visuals may blend over at most ~0.2 seconds without delaying that transition. Do not wait for chewing, sleep, a bath exit flourish, a pet response or a celebration to finish. Clear props safely if a normal return-to-table gesture cannot run.

Every scene owns its temporary prop group, overlays, animal targeting, particles, pose overrides and station reservation. Cleanup must be idempotent for interruption, resident removal, route failure, floor/tier/layout rebuild, switching home and disposal. Restore fixture materials/visibility and seeded centerpiece state, normal eye/body visibility, root rotation/height and all limb targets. Remove only this scene's resources; preserve shared materials, existing pets/furniture, user decor, saved outfit and other residents' scenes. A stale scene must not reattach its props or signal completion after cancellation. Re-evaluate the new layout before any later activity.

Ordinary leisure is always simulation. Present its label as “<activity> · simulated” in activity detail; preserve the observed status bubble and plumbob semantics. Off duty remains “no session”, even when the character paints or sleeps. Do not use a finished provider turn, participation XP, a timer, an animation endpoint or a smiling face as evidence of successful work.

Goal, award, achievement and praise rows are explicitly staged simulation, with the exact scene wording above. Their shapes must not resemble the real acceptance/reward UI, and they never write task status, outcome, XP, gems, provider events or acceptance. Existing actual human care actions may keep their own receipts/happiness changes; attaching a visual neither duplicates nor expands those rules. Ambient leisure grants no new happiness. Do not add care API calls or synthetic observations just to demonstrate these scenes.
